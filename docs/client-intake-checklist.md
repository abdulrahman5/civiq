# Client intake checklist

Send this to a new legislator's office at the start of onboarding. Everything on their Civiq website comes from it. Ask them to put it all in one shared folder (photos as original files, not WhatsApp-compressed copies).

Used in step 2 of [Launching a new legislator site](launching-a-new-site.md).

---

## Identity
- [ ] **Full name and title** as it should appear, e.g. *Hon. Ada Okafor*
- [ ] **Short name** for the logo, e.g. *ADA* (2–5 letters)
- [ ] **Party**
- [ ] **Office**, e.g. *Member, House of Representatives* or *Senator*
- [ ] **Constituency** and **state**
- [ ] **Domain** they own or want, e.g. *adaokafor.ng*

## Brand
- [ ] **Main colour** and **accent colour** (party colours are fine), or "Civiq to choose"
  - The main colour sits behind white text, so it must be fairly dark.

## Photos
- [ ] **Official portrait**: upright, well lit, face clearly visible, at least 1200 px tall
- [ ] **Wide image** for link previews when the site is shared (ideally 1200×630)
- [ ] **About page photo** (can be the portrait)
- [ ] **Project and event photos**, each with a one-line caption

## Home page
- [ ] **Headline**, e.g. *Delivering for Anambra*
- [ ] **Words to highlight** in the accent colour (optional)
- [ ] **Intro paragraph** (2–3 sentences)
- [ ] **Badge**, e.g. *APC · Anambra South* (optional)
- [ ] **3–4 key numbers** with labels, e.g. *12 bills sponsored*, *40 boreholes*
- [ ] **Signature quote** (optional)

## About
- [ ] **Biography** (300–600 words)
- [ ] **Quick facts**: education, committees, previous roles, etc.
- [ ] **Timeline** of milestones: year, title and one line each

## Agenda
- [ ] **4–6 priorities**, each with a title and a one-sentence description, e.g. *Water access: a borehole in every ward by 2027*

## Projects and news
- [ ] **Completed projects**: title, location, date, short description, photos
- [ ] **2–3 recent news items or press releases**, with photos

## Contact
- [ ] **Office postal address** (required: newsletters must include it)
- [ ] **Public email** for the office (replies to newsletters and messages go here)
- [ ] **Phone / WhatsApp** number to show on the site
- [ ] **Email address for contact-form alerts** (who should be told when someone writes in)
- [ ] **Privacy contact email** (can be the same as the public email)

## Social media
- [ ] Links to **Facebook**, **X**, **Instagram**, **YouTube** and **TikTok** (whichever they use)

## Team
For each person who will use the Civiq Portal:
- [ ] **Name**, **email** and **role**:
  - *Admin*: everything, including managing the team (usually the chief of staff or main contact)
  - *Editor*: website, community, inbox and newsletters
  - *Contributor*: writes drafts only
